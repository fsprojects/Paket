InstallModel Type

Namespace: Paket
Assembly: Paket.Core.dll
Base Type: obj
All Interfaces: IEquatable<InstallModel> , IStructuralEquatable , IComparable<InstallModel> , IComparable , IStructuralComparable

Represents the contents of a particular package at a particular version. Any install-specific actions like Content files, References, Roslyn Analyzers, MsBuild targets are represented here.
